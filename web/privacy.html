Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026 · App: Arrow Escape (com.developerarslanali.arrowescape)

1. Overview

Arrow Escape is developed by Developer Arslan Ali as an offline-first single-player puzzle game. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, secure gaming experience.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Arrow Escape does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identifying information (PII). Specifically:

  • No accounts, emails, or personal credentials are required.
  • No location tracking, GPS, or IP address logging.
  • No contact list, photos, camera, or microphone access.
  • No behavioral tracking or advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA).

3. Local Device Storage Only

All game progression (unlocked levels, star ratings, best moves, and sound settings) is saved strictly on your local device storage using Godot's sandboxed atomic write protocol. This data never leaves your device and is erased if you uninstall the application.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The standard release build of Arrow Escape operates 100% offline and does not bundle third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase) or advertising trackers. Any future optional rewarded services will strictly require explicit user consent.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Arrow Escape is family-friendly and rated for Everyone (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone). Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we comply fully with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.
