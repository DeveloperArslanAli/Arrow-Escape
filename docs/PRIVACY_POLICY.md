# Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026  
**Developer:** Developer Arslan Ali  
**Application:** Arrow Escape (Package: `com.developerarslanali.arrowescape`)

---

## 1. Overview
At **Arrow Escape**, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the mobile application *Arrow Escape*.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
*Arrow Escape* is designed as an offline single-player puzzle game.
- **Personal Data:** We do **NOT** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers).
- **Analytics & Tracking:** We do **NOT** integrate third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or cookies.
- **Financial Information:** *Arrow Escape* does **NOT** process payments, transactions, or in-app purchases.

## 3. Local Device Storage
All gameplay progress (such as unlocked levels, completed stars, and player settings like audio toggles) is stored locally on your device using Godot's sandboxed local user data directory (`user://save_data.json`). This data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the application.

## 4. Permissions
*Arrow Escape* requires minimal permissions:
- `VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile feedback during arrow tap interactions and puzzle completion. It does not access private data or system functions.
- `INTERNET`: Disabled / Not required.

## 5. Children's Privacy
*Arrow Escape* is suitable for all ages (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone). Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
